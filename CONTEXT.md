Public site at `semlay.com`. Primary conversion: start a free **Readiness Check** at `/check` (intake survey + schema upload when live).

**Landing Page Primary CTA**:
Button label **Get your free readiness report** — links to `/check`. Secondary line: schema upload, results in minutes, no sales call.
_Avoid_: Book readiness audit, half-day engagement copy
